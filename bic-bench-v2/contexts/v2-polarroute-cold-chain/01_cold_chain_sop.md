# SOP CC-12 — continuous temperature evidence

**Product class:** refrigerated biologics, 2–8 °C

| Evidence condition | WMS state | Next authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continuous record, 2–8 °C | Acceptance eligible | Operations |
| Missing or unreadable interval up to 15 minutes with supporting evidence | Quality review if requested | Quality |
| Missing or unreadable interval over 15 minutes | Quality hold / telemetry continuity exception | Quality disposition |

Endpoint readings do not fill a missing interval. Customer schedules may be stricter but do not lower this matrix.
