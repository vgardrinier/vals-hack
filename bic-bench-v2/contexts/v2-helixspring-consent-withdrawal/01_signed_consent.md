# HS consent Section 11 scope ledger

| Asset state at withdrawal | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Remaining coded specimen | Destroy |
| Identifiable or linked research data | Stop future use and remove linkage |
| De-identified value already pooled in locked aggregate | Not extractable from aggregate |
| Withdrawal and destruction audit evidence | Retain under compliance schedule |

The participant-specific linkage key is handled separately from the locked aggregate.
