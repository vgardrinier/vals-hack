# Permit desk bulletin — Revision 6

The lift-plan calculator assigns class C2 when chart utilization exceeds 75% or
the load crosses an occupied structure. Other planned picks are C1. Field notes
may alter sequencing but do not change the calculator's class.

Annex W permits SITE-MET readings for C1. A C2 permit packet instead references
the source mounted at the crane's operating height. Replacing that source creates
a new equipment event: its certificate, live functional result, and issuer
acknowledgment must all belong to the same event before the packet can advance.
An installation photograph closes none of those fields. Ground shielding is
already included in the C1 tolerance and is not an alternate C2 method.
