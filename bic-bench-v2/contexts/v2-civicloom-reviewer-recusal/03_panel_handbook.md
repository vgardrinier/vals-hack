# Panel relationship matrix

| Disclosed relationship | Applicant linkage | Assignment state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fiduciary role | Applicant | Recuse |
| Fiduciary role | Parent or controlling member | Recuse |
| Fiduciary role | Fiscal sponsor | Recuse |
| Subject-matter familiarity only | None | Eligible |

The applicant governance attachment, rather than name matching, determines the linkage column. Recusal is recorded before panel assignment.
