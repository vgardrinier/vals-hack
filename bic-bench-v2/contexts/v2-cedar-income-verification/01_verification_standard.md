# Retail Lending Standard 6.4 — employment and income

**Effective:** 2026-07-15  
**Owner:** Credit Risk

A verbal verification of employment may establish employer, active status, start date, and job title. A verbal statement of compensation must not be used to set `income_status = verified`.

Verified income requires one of the following: recent pay statements, a tax transcript where applicable, or a written compensation statement returned directly by the employer through the approved verification portal. The evidence source must be recorded in the CRM.
