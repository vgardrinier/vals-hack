# External incident communications standard

**Owner:** Trust Operations  
**Effective:** 2026-06-10

Customer-facing channels may describe verified service symptoms, affected regions, mitigation work, and investigation status at any time.

Only the active Incident Commander may classify an event publicly as a security incident or attribute a security cause. Until that role records a classification, use operational language such as “elevated login failures” or “authentication disruption under investigation.” Do not use breach, attack, compromise, credential stuffing, or similar causal labels as facts.
