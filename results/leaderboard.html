Can AI agents follow the rules nobody wrote down?

Each agent joins the finance team at a simulated AI company, gets five new supplier invoices and three months of company email, Slack and tickets. Which invoices need the finance director's approval was never written down: the cutoff moved from $5,000 to $10,000 in October, and one supplier stopped needing approval after signing a contract. The only way to know is to read how the team actually worked.
