Intermittent p99 tail-latency spikes on dedicated cluster due to NVLink daisychain contention and long-lived small streams

Issue summary: Customer (NexaHealth) reports intermittent p99 tail-latency spikes during sustained traffic windows on their dedicated cluster. Impact: user-facing generation calls observe occasional 5-15s tail latencies causing application timeouts and degraded UX. Hypothesis: contention across NVLink connection chain (daisychain topology) combined with a surge of many long-lived, low-throughput streams is causing backend pipeline stalls and autoscaler misclassification. Environment: dedicated pool with mixed A100-40GB and A100-80GB nodes; placements have resulted in multi-hop NVLink paths for some tenants.
Several customers' requests hit p99 > 10s intermittently; throughput for pinned cluster dipped ~30% during events; customer reports production errors and increased retries leading to higher billable retries and degraded SLOs.
1) Route sustained traffic of many small streaming sessions (1-5 tokens per request) for vicuna-13b pinned to the dedicated pool
2) Mix in a few long-lived low-CPU streams (open WS for 10-20s) to simulate chat sessions
3) Observe tail-latency metrics on per-tenant dashboard and GPU perf counters
4) Trigger autoscaler test by artificially increasing ephemeral stream count by ~40% over baseline
