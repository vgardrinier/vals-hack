LatticeWorks embeddings quality degradation causing multi-region failover and billing exposure - customer escalated

Issue summary: Starting 2024-11-07 14:05 UTC, LatticeWorks observed a sharp drop in embeddings similarity scores and a corresponding spike in 5xx errors when indexing new vectors. Traffic from their production clusters in us-east and eu-west triggered automated routing failovers to fallback embeddings variants, increasing latency and unit costs. Impact: Search relevance degraded across customer-facing features (recommendations, semantic search). Customer reported missing results and billing anomalies. Scope: Affected only LatticeWorks tenant on enterprise plan; cross-region failover caused billing exposure for both regions as requests were routed to quantized fallback models.
Timeline: Incident began at 14:05 UTC when bulk ingestion peaked. Automated fallback rules engaged within 30-90s.
Finding 1: Kernel-selection PR #1987 introduced a race when batch_size > 1024 resulting in kernel negotiation failures (KERN-503) causing routing to pick quantized fallback variants.
Finding 2: embed-quant-1 has distributional shift vs open-embed-qa-2.1 leading to low cosine similarity on LatticeWorks test vectors; quality regression not previously flagged by model onboarding tests.
Finding 3: Autoscaler heat-up + high eviction rate created pod churn, amplifying kernel negotiation failures.
Finding 4: Billing pipeline counted routed fallback requests at higher unit-cost; customer saw a preliminary invoice preview spike (~+34%).
Partial mitigations applied: PR revert, restart of affected pools, temporary ingestion rate-limits for customer key, billing hold placed pending review.
Monitoring gaps: No synthetic check that verifies similarity distribution after kernel change; routing tests only validated latency and error rates, not embedding quality.
Hypothesis validated by local repro and metric correlation graphs (kernel_error_rate vs fallback_rate vs cosine_mean).
Root cause: A kernel-selection change (PR #1987) created a failure path under large-batch ingest which triggered automated routing to quantized fallback variants. The quantized variant produced lower-quality embeddings for LatticeWorks' comparator set and increased billing. Fix: reverted PR #1987, restarted affected serving pools, enforced ingestion rate-limits for LatticeWorks, and held billing for impacted window. Deployed a hotfix to make kernel negotiation tolerant to large-batches and added a feature-flag gate for the kernel rollout. Customer was issued a credit equivalent to estimated excess charge and notified of the post-incident plan.
