CobaltGrocer: prolonged tail latency and streaming RESETs during peak-hour checkout flows

Issue summary: During CobaltGrocer's weekday afternoon peak (13:00–15:00 PST) customers reported elevated completion tail latency and increased streaming connection resets for checkout conversational flows. Impact: checkout chat completions intermittently truncated or failed to stream, increasing abandoned carts and urgent escalation from customer engineering. Environment: prod us-west-2, dedicated routing policy 'enterprise-usw2', traffic routed to dedicated GPU pool 'dg-pool-31'.
Multiple merchant checkout conversations experienced either abrupt streaming RESETs mid-completion or completions taking 2–6s longer than SLA 200ms p95 on tail tokens. Customer observed a 4.8% increase in failed/aborted checkout events during the window and reported immediate business impact (failed payments & increased support calls).

Hypotheses: 1) Kernel selector update (attention-opt-v2) increased PCIe transfer contention on a subset of nodes causing KV-cache stalls and streaming resets. 2) Autoscaler oscillation briefly pushed traffic onto under-provisioned nodes exposing a noisy neighbor effect. Findings: profiling showed attention-opt-v2 increased aggregate device-to-host transfers for certain sequence-length distributions used by CobaltGrocer checkout prompts. Nodes with slightly older firmware experienced PCIe throttling leading to stalls recorded in perf-agent. Correlation between scale events and surge in RST_STREAMs confirmed traffic shifting amplified failures.
- Immediately applied temporary routing to fallback quantized variant (reduced device-to-host transfers).
- Rolled back kernel selector change from attention-opt-v2 to attention-opt-v1 on dg-pool-31.
- Patched autoscaler cooldown to avoid sub-30s oscillations for dedicated pools.
- Opened firmware update roll for dg-node-31a/31b with infra team.
- Increased observability retention for relevant traces by 7 days to assist postmortem.
Root cause: combination of a recent kernel selector change that increased PCIe activity for long-chat sequences and autoscaler oscillation shifting traffic to nodes with firmware-induced PCIe throttling. Resolution steps: rolled back kernel selector in affected pool (2026-03-10 13:05 UTC), adjusted autoscaler cooldown (2026-03-10 13:22 UTC), and scheduled firmware updates on two nodes. Customer validated that streaming stability and tail latency returned to acceptable ranges by 14:30 UTC. No code-level rollback required beyond selector config. Incident declared resolved after 3-hour monitoring window.
