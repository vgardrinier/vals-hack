Stale hourly-tag led to missing invoice line for reserved+hosted hybrid batch embedding run

Issue summary: Customer reports that a large multi-region batch embedding job on 2026-03-08 was partially billed, with an entire hourly invoice line missing for ~2.5 hours of hosted fallback usage. The job ran on Storlium's dedicated pool with automatic fallback to hosted in eu-west due to transient capacity constraints. Impact: customer expects reserved-rate credits to apply for the reserved portion and a hosted line item for the fallback hours; instead the hosted fallback hours are missing from the invoice, resulting in an under-invoiced period and incorrect credit application. This has caused Storlium to report reconciliation gaps against their internal spend dashboard and they request an adjustment and explanation.
Storlium Analytics reports a missing invoice line for ~2.5 hours of hosted fallback usage during a hybrid reserved-hosted job. Financial reconciliation gap of ~$1,200 and inability to map usage to forecasted reserved capacity - customer is enterprise/dedicated and flagged as P1.

Initial triage: reconciler emitted warning about hour_tag mismatch; postbacks for affected interval ingested but grouped under stale hour index, causing ledger generator to drop group during invoice-line emission step.
Hypothesis: race between postback-indexer replication and reconciler consumer left a stale hour_tag value in cache for org 88a2. When ledger job grouped by hour_window_id it saw a tag from the previous day and excluded it due to dedup guard (to prevent double-billing).
Evidence: reconciler log shows 'expected_tag != current_tag' and the hourly index dump contains two different hour_window_id entries for the same token batch; postback timestamps match the hosted fallback period.
Repro attempt: reprocessing the exact postback batch through a local reconciler replayer yields proper emission of invoice line when hour_tag is fixed to the correct value; when left stale the line is omitted.
Scope: initial scan shows only this org and one other small customer had similar pattern in the same time window; likely related to a replication lag event in the indexer on 2026-03-08 09:58-10:20 UTC.
Risk assessment: low likelihood of broad account impact but medium severity for affected customers due to billing correctness.
A stale hour_tag value in the hourly index for org 88a2 (Storlium) caused the postbacks from hosted fallback microbatches to be grouped under a tag that the ledger emitter considered already-processed. The deduplication guard therefore suppressed emission of a hosted invoice line for the affected hour window.
Billing team performed a manual ledger re-run for the affected hours and emitted a credit memo for the missing hosted fallback usage. A manual invoice adjustment will be visible on Storlium's billing dashboard and a credit memo email sent by 2026-03-16. Reconciler and indexer teams deployed a hotfix to ensure the postback-indexer updates hour_tag atomically and added an alert for hour_tag replication lag above 1 minute.
