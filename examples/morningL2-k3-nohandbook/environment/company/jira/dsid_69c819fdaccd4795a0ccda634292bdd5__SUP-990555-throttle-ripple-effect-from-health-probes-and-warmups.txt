Throttle ripple effect from health probes + warmups causing intermittent 429 waves

Issue summary: NimbusSearch reports recurring short 'waves' of 429 throttles affecting multiple routes and API keys within their dedicated pool. The pattern correlates with scheduled health probes and model warmup spikes from their ingestion fleet. Impact: Several customer-critical search ingestion jobs retry/fail, causing lag and backfill delays (customer reports ~20-30% of concurrent batches hitting 429 for 30-90s windows). Environment: prod/us-west (dedicated pool: dp-54321).
1) Deploy 50 concurrent ingesters each running scheduled warmup + health probe (10s interval, no jitter) against /v1/embeddings endpoint.
2) Start a bulk import job that streams batches of 512 items per request (parallelism=40).
3) Observe 429s returned for a subset of requests; 429 bursts align with probe timestamps and warmup start times.
4) Re-run with probes disabled or with randomized jitter (0-30s) and observe 429s drop significantly.
