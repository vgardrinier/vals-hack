# LO-71 executed order — milestone 4

The final twenty percent becomes due upon Acceptance. For this milestone,
Acceptance means written confirmation from the Designated Approver identified in
the executed Project Charter, or from a delegate entered in the charter before
the confirmation is given. Project correspondence is not an amendment to that
designation.

Technical completion starts a five-business-day review period. Silence can have
the effect stated in this paragraph only after that period has elapsed and only
if the customer has raised no defect. The delivery team may close its technical
checklist before Acceptance; that operational event does not itself make the
final amount due.
