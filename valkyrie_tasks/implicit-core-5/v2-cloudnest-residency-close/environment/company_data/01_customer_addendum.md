# CN-EU44 addendum excerpts

Section 2 limits the Processing Location for Customer Content to the EEA.
Section 1.4 defines Customer Content to include every copy from which a customer
record can be reconstructed, whether or not that copy serves production traffic.
The definition excludes telemetry that cannot restore customer records.

Schedule D says the migration certificate is populated from the service's
restoration catalog. The product console is listed only as evidence for the
primary-processing line item. A location stops appearing on the certificate
after its last recoverable generation is retired, not when traffic reaches zero.
