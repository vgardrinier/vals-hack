# CN-EU44 preservation mapping

Audit matter A-902 lists `snap-188` and `snap-194` as preservation sources through 2026-12-02. The preservation owner requires a verified bit-equivalent successor copy before a source location is made non-restorable. A Frankfurt successor satisfies preservation if its checksum and restore test are attached to the matter.

The matter does not authorize keeping an additional restorable copy in another geography after successor verification.
