# Workspace boundary index — excerpt

`C0` means that the owning queue has not entered a disposition. Until it does,
the handling column controls. `H-4` material may cross into boundary B3 with its
R-list intact and the external ACL empty. A checksum may leave B3. File content
may not.

RE-2 is boundary B3. The partner's named-user group is considered external even
inside RE-2 and is enabled only after an external-release event. The general
collaboration workspace is boundary B1; B1 accepts dispositions C2 and C3, not
C0/H-4. Encrypted staging in B3 does not predict the eventual disposition.
